# Józsefváros in 33 photos: past and present

A picture archive for giving Nyócker a more documentary, district-rooted look.
26 archive photos from **Fortepan** (1875–1989) and 7 present-day photos from **Wikimedia Commons** (2015–2024).
Picked November 2026.

## Using them

- **Fortepan** photos are free to use, including commercially, under **CC BY-SA 3.0**. Credit each one exactly as Fortepan asks: `Fortepan / <donor>` (the donor is in the Credit column).
- **Wikimedia Commons** photos carry their own licence, shown per photo. CC BY-SA means: name the author, link the licence, and share changes under the same licence. CC0 needs no credit, but giving one is good practice.
- Full-size files: on each Fortepan page use "Download photo"; on Commons, "Download".

## Notes

- Dates are as the archives give them. Fortepan files the Népszínház photo under 1900, but its own description dates it to about 1875.
- The 1956 sequence (14–17) and the 1945 ruins (11–12) are the district's turning points. They are worth giving room, not cropping into thumbnails.
- Pairs that tell a before and after: Népszínház 1875 → demolition 1965 → Blaha rebuilt 2023 (1, 19, 31); Corvin Áruház 1930 → 1977 → 2021 (6, 22, 29); Keleti 1895 → 1987 → 2015 (2, 24, 27); market hall 1946 → 2024 (13, 33).
